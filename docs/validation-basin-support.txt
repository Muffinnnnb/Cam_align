Mechanism and safeguards
-----------------------
The remaining v12 failures were fragmented source supports. Independent depth
footprints seed a watershed of range-gradient cost, with 5/9-pixel median
proposal scales and search radii proportional to footprint size. Unsaturated
16-bit gradient ordering avoids artificial constant-cost plateaus. Source
proposals overlapping an already eligible object are skipped; new candidates
are appended after the old paths rather than changing their priority.

The image border and artificial search fence are NOT physical board edges.
A final candidate must also have a measured outer neighborhood inconsistent
with its own plane: >=55% of valid surrounding points beyond the existing
30 mm source-outlier scale, with >=20 samples. An inner gap of 5% of the
footprint's linear image scale (minimum 2 pixels), then an equally wide outer
ring, avoids the fitted support's eroded rim and mixed boundary pixels.
This is SOURCE plane continuation, not an assumption about camera separation.
Missing ring points do not count as positive boundary evidence. A board flush
with a coplanar background may remain unresolved by this supplemental path;
the previous detection paths remain intact.

Median filtering and nearest-filled proposal images do NOT modify measured
XYZ. The final support, normal, size and PLY use original finite samples via
the unchanged complete_support and support_quality routines. Existing size,
flatness, occupancy, quadrilateral, normal-stability and identity gates were
not relaxed. No manual ROI, timestamp exception, fixed pixel location, RGB
crop, missing-checker-pattern rule or presumed sensor baseline is used.
The known 250x280 mm target and organized, correctly modelled XYZ remain
explicit prerequisites. This is not a universal detector of arbitrary boards.
RANSAC, linear-loss LM, source-only dimension fitting and fusion are unchanged.
Range-basin supplements have calibration_eligible=False: they are identity
proposals, not RT/dimension observations. A broader bounded footprint is not
proof of an unbiased calibration normal. board_candidates(for_calibration=True)
keeps the previous observations and ordering; display association still examines
the new source candidates. This is a method-level policy, not a frame exception.
