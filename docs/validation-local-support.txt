What changed, and why it is not a frame-specific workaround
----------------------------------------------------------
Scene-wide hypotheses can be dominated by larger surfaces. Existing incomplete
SOURCE objects with independent boundary support propose locations for metric
local fits. 50 mm voxels choose starting samples; 60/100/160 mm neighborhoods
of original points generate local plane hypotheses. Their plane-residual
regions are finally refit and tested using original finite XYZ.

Existing eligible objects are not replaced. Extra candidates are appended,
not re-sorted ahead of the original hypotheses. The selected final support
must pass the UNCHANGED size, flatness, occupancy, boundary, quadrilateral and
normal-stability checks, then the existing finite-board association rules.
There is no RGB/RT-guided source crop, fixed image position, timestamp rule,
checker-pattern/missing-depth heuristic, presumed baseline, or synthetic XYZ.
The known 250x280 mm board remains an explicit target assumption.
RANSAC thresholds, source-only dimension estimation, and linear-loss LM remain
unchanged. Source masks never force colors or move points during RGB fusion.

For speed, the large residual median uses 1 mm quantization of PROPOSALS only;
original measured coordinates and final fits/exports are not quantized.
Exact bit-set IoU replaces repeated full-grid overlap scans. On ten evenly
spaced cached frames, old/new local-fallback masks and normals were identical.
Mean supplemental cost: 6.02 -> 5.35 seconds in a concurrent-test session;
this is NOT a reliable standalone FPS benchmark. More search still costs time.
Selected-frame analysis now runs in a bounded background worker. Latest
selection wins, stale results may be cached but cannot replace the view, and
read/association failures are logged without entering an automatic retry loop.
